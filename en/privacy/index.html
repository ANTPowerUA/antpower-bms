ANTPower BMS Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Who is responsible for the app

The ANTPower BMS app for iPhone and Android is developed and published by Myroslav Zaitsev, an individual developer based in Ukraine. For privacy questions, email bmshelp741@gmail.com.

In short

The app does not collect your data and does not share it with anyone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cloud services and no third-party libraries that collect data. Everything the app works with stays on your phone.

What is stored on your phone

So that you don't have to enter the same things every time, the app keeps the following in its own storage on your phone:

  • BMS boards you have connected to: board name and Bluetooth address, and the last connected board;
  • pairing passwords you entered for your boards;
  • battery groups you created: name, type and members;
  • the app language and your answers to permission prompts.

This data is stored only on your phone and, if you have phone backup turned on (iCloud or Google), in your own backup. The developer never receives it. It is protected by your phone's operating system: other apps cannot access it. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Board data

The app reads the BMS board's status (voltage, current, state of charge, temperatures, cell voltages), settings and logs over Bluetooth and shows them on screen. This data is not sent to the developer or anyone else either.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth — to find your BMS board and exchange data with it.
  • Camera — only to scan the board's QR code. Images are neither stored nor sent anywhere.
  • Location (Android only) — Android requires this permission to search for Bluetooth devices. The app does not determine, store or send your location.

You can revoke any permission in your phone's settings. Without Bluetooth the app cannot connect to the board.

Files you share

You can save a board log or settings file and share it through the system Share menu. The file goes only where you send it. The developer receives it only if you send it to the developer, for example when asking for support.

Children

The app is intended for configuring battery systems and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts transmitting any data, the developer will update this policy before that version is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: bmshelp741@gmail.com.
