ANTPower BMS

An app for monitoring and configuring battery BMS boards over Bluetooth.

What the app does

  • shows voltage, current, state of charge, power, temperatures and the voltage of every cell;
  • changes board settings after you enter the password;
  • shows board logs and saves them to a file;
  • saves board settings to a file and writes them back to the board from a file;
  • combines several batteries into a group;
  • works with the external LED display.

Compatible boards

JK (Jikong) BMS boards with a Bluetooth module. The app runs on iPhone and Android.

FAQ

The app cannot find my board

Make sure Bluetooth is on and the app is allowed to use it (on Android, location permission too). A board works with one phone at a time: close the app on any other phone.

On iPhone the connection drops when I leave the app

This is by design: the board is released so that another phone can connect. Go back to the app and select the board again — you will not need to re-enter the password.

The board asks for a password

This is the pairing password stored in the board itself. If you don't know it, contact the seller of your battery.
